About Us

Turning clean energy into food security

Climate Nanos Enterprise was founded on December 2, 2024 to solve one of rural Africa's most costly problems: post-harvest loss. Across Nigeria, farmers lose a large share of their perishable harvest — tomatoes, peppers and vegetables — within days, simply because they have no reliable power for cooling and preservation.

Our answer is off-grid clean energy microgrids combined with nanotechnology and IoT. Through our flagship platform, Smart Solar Agri-Hub, we bring solar-powered preservation, smart sensors and nano-enabled inputs directly to the farm gate — extending shelf life, raising incomes and lowering emissions.

Headquartered in Alabata, Abeokuta, Ogun State, we are a multidisciplinary team of engineers, innovators and agribusiness professionals driving sustainable food systems across Africa — empowering youth and energizing agri-business in Nigeria.
