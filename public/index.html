Enterprise Overview

Empowering Youth and Energizing Agribusiness

Founded on **December 2, 2024** and officially incorporated under CAC (BN 9703072), Climate Nanos Enterprise tackles Sub-Saharan Africa’s critical post-harvest crisis where 30% to 50% of fresh produce rots within 48 hours due to unreliable cold chains and grid instability[cite: 2, 3, 5, 6].

Operating from Ogun State, Nigeria, our mission is to deploy scalable decentralized infrastructure that bridges smallholder farmers directly with urban digital markets while certifying sustainability through automated carbon telemetry[cite: 2, 4].

Scalable Architecture

The Tri-Integration Model

Combining physical hardware, digital e-commerce, and Adaption AI carbon auditing.

⚡

1. Hardware Innovation

Zero-carbon cold rooms (R-290/R-600a refrigerants), automated solenoid drip irrigation, and PIR motion-defense perimeters extending shelf life from 2 to 14+ days[cite: 3, 7, 8].

🛒

2. Digital E-Commerce

Direct farm-to-consumer digital marketplace connecting rural cold hubs to urban off-takers and international processors via seamless transaction APIs[cite: 3, 4, 9].

🛡️

3. Adaption AI (Green Stability Matrix)

Automated CO₂ lifecycle auditing via n9n pipelines that evaluate supply chain telemetry, calculate emissions in kg CO₂e, and inject certified green badges onto product listings[cite: 3, 9, 15].
