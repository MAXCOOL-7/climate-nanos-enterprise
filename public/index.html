Enterprise Overview

Turning Clean Energy into Global Food Security

Founded on December 2, 2024, Climate Nanos Enterprise addresses Africa’s critical post-harvest challenge: over 40% of fresh produce spoils before reaching market due to severe cold-chain infrastructure deficits.

Our solution deploys off-grid clean energy microgrids integrated with low-pressure IoT telemetry and nanotechnology. Our flagship platform, the Smart Solar Agri-Hub, delivers on-farm preservation, real-time sensor analytics, and precision yield protection directly to rural agricultural clusters.
